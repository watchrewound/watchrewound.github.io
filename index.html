Interactive 3D horror stories. Watch the wrong choice, then rewind.

About

Rewound is an original animated horror series published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Each episode shows a wrong choice and its consequences, then rewinds to continue the story down the right path.

About the Rewound Pipeline app

Rewound Pipeline is a private tool used only by the Rewound creator to upload Rewound's own original videos to the Rewound YouTube channel, after each video is manually reviewed and approved. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user.
